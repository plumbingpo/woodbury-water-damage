Flood Prevention for Homeowners in Woodbury, MN

Home / Blog / Flood Damage Preparation Tips in Woodbury, MN

Nobody controls the weather, yet Woodbury, MN property owners can still get ahead of flood damage with a few proactive steps. Being prepared significantly softens the blow of a flood and may save you thousands on restoration. Below, our Woodbury Restoration team lays out what Woodbury homeowners can do to prepare most effectively.

Find Out How Flood-Prone You Are

Preparation begins with knowing how likely your property is to flood. Check FEMA flood maps to determine whether your property is in a designated flood zone. Keep in mind that the majority of flood claims come from areas not designated as flood zones, so a lower-risk location still deserves attention. Consider drainage patterns, the lay of the land, and the distance to creeks or other waterways that heavy rain could push over their banks.

Protect Your Property

Key physical preparations include ensuring proper grading slopes away from your foundation, keeping gutters and downspouts clear and extending discharge at least 6 feet from the foundation, installing and maintaining a sump pump with battery backup, sealing foundation cracks and applying waterproof coatings, elevating electrical panels and HVAC equipment above potential flood levels, and storing important documents and valuables above ground level.

Prepare Your Insurance Ahead of Time

Standard homeowner's insurance does not cover flood damage from rising water. Flood policies are sold by the NFIP and by private carriers, and they usually come with a 30-day wait before coverage begins, so look into one long before any forecast forces the issue. Photograph and film your home and its contents so you have a record for the insurer. Keep insurance documents accessible and know your coverage limits and deductible.

Your Plan for a Flood Emergency

Planning has to happen before floodwater is on the way. Know your evacuation routes and have a go-bag ready. Keep Woodbury Restoration's number ((760) 542-1883) in your phone for immediate post-flood professional cleanup. Have your insurance agent's contact information readily available. When a flood is in the forecast, carry valuables upstairs, shut off utilities if you are told to, and record the condition of your property before it arrives.
