Water Extraction

Home Water Extraction and Removal

A broken pipe, a leaking appliance, or a storm can flood a house fast, and extraction by professionals remains the safest answer and the fastest one. From a small leak to a fully flooded floor, Woodbury Restoration removes water from homes across Woodbury. On each job we assess the damage, pump out the water with industrial equipment, map the moisture, protect your belongings, and document everything for your insurer.

Water left sitting indoors threatens the house itself, everything in it, and the people who live there. It takes only hours for drywall to swell, hardwood to warp, and electrical components to start corroding. If left untreated for 24 to 48 hours, mold growth becomes almost inevitable. Even a small puddle that should not be there is reason enough to call a professional right away. The sooner we arrive, the sooner we can measure what is wet and get extraction going, ahead of any secondary damage.

Hidden leaks that have dripped for days or weeks are part of our work too. Long before anything shows on the surface, a slow drip under flooring or inside a wall can do serious harm to the structure. Our technicians use thermal imaging cameras and professional moisture meters to locate hidden moisture pockets and ensure complete drying.

Complete Dry-Out

Moisture Monitoring and Structural Drying

Complete structural drying is the most critical step in preventing long-term damage to your home after water intrusion. Done right, it pulls trapped moisture out of subfloors, wall cavities, and framing, keeps mold from starting, and returns the structure to sound condition. We take and log moisture readings daily, and each Woodbury Restoration drying job is run according to IICRC S500.

Our residential drying setup covers high-velocity air movers, commercial dehumidifiers, specialty systems made for wall cavities and hardwood floors, plus thermal imaging to keep verifying moisture as we go. No two houses dry the same way, so we build the plan around your layout, your building materials, and how bad the damage is.

Homeowners are often surprised by how thorough professional drying needs to be. Even after visible water is gone, moisture trapped in wall cavities, under flooring, and in structural framing can fuel mold growth and cause wood rot for weeks. Our team checks moisture each day and only signs off once every measurement matches what it was before the loss. Depending on how much got wet, drying a home usually takes 3 to 5 days.

Stopping Mold

Home Mold Remediation and Prevention

Mold can begin growing within 24 to 48 hours of water exposure, spreading rapidly through your home and creating serious health risks for your family. If mold is already growing, Woodbury Restoration can remediate it as a standalone service, and prevention is built into each restoration project we do. Treatment is carried out safely and thoroughly by IICRC-certified technicians working to IICRC S520 and EPA protocols.

Any time a home has water damage, our advice is to include a mold prevention treatment. Applying antimicrobial agents during the drying process is far more effective and cost-efficient than treating mold growth after it has taken hold. However, if you already have visible mold or suspect hidden mold from a previous water event, we provide comprehensive testing, containment, removal, and post-remediation verification to ensure your home is safe and healthy.

Insurance Support

Help With Your Woodbury Water Damage Claim

Insurance paperwork is the last thing most people want to face while part of their home is unusable. Woodbury Restoration works directly with all major insurance carriers to streamline the claims process for you. Photos, readings from our moisture meters, the materials that were affected, and the work we perform all go into a complete report that satisfies what insurance companies ask for.

Having worked through claims with thousands of homeowners in Woodbury, we know what an adjuster looks for before approving coverage. Clear records and a direct line to the insurance company usually mean less back-and-forth, quicker processing, and a claim that captures everything you lost. We're happy to explain your coverage, answer questions, and advocate on your behalf throughout the entire restoration process.

In most cases a homeowner policy covers water damage that is sudden and accidental, for example storm intrusion, burst pipes, or a failed appliance. We can go through your policy with you so you know what applies and can file a complete claim. Compared with filing on your own, paying a little for professional documentation frequently leads to a significantly higher insurance payout.

Sewage Cleanup

Cleaning Up After a Sewer Backup

Sewage backups are among the most hazardous water damage events a homeowner can face. This is Category 3 black water, and the viruses, bacteria, and other pathogens in it are a serious health risk. Contaminated water calls for strict IICRC protocols, and the Woodbury Restoration crew has the specialized training and equipment to follow them safely.

The work runs in six steps: sealing off the area, safely pumping out the sewage, hauling away materials beyond saving, antimicrobial cleaning, full structural drying, and a final test confirming the house is safe. We handle the entire process from start to finish so you and your family can return to a clean, healthy home as quickly as possible.
