Few emergencies in a building are as hazardous as sewage backing up into it. Pathogens in raw sewage include salmonella, giardia, hepatitis and E. coli, along with other viruses, parasites and bacteria, and contact with anything contaminated is a serious risk to health. Across Woodbury, Woodbury Restoration answers sewage emergencies 24/7, sending trained technicians who arrive with EPA-registered disinfectants and the right protective equipment so your property is decontaminated safely.

Sewage damage is classified as Category 3 (black water), the most contaminated and dangerous category of water damage. That rating calls for the strictest protocols: containment barriers go up, crews wear full protective equipment, every porous material the sewage touched is removed, and the surfaces that stay are disinfected thoroughly. We work to the IICRC S500 standard that governs Category 3 losses, with the goal of returning your property to a condition safe to occupy.

We clear the standing sewage first, then pull contaminated water out of structural materials, remove carpet, padding, insulation, drywall and any other porous item that was in contact with sewage for proper disposal, disinfect each hard surface, treat with antimicrobial products and finish with structural drying, which prevents secondary mold. To finish, thermal foggers and hydroxyl generators of industrial grade deodorize the space so the sewage smell does not linger.

Sewage Cleanup Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Roots from trees close to the sewer line work their way into pipes and block them, so have them cleared
  • Fit a strainer over each sink and shower drain so hair and debris stay out of the pipes
  • Every 2 to 3 years, get a sewer camera run through the line to spot developing issues
  • Keep cooking oil, grease and food scraps out of the drain, where they build into clogs
  • Route sump pump and gutter water away from the sewer line, which can back up when storm flow overloads it
  • A backwater valve at the sewer connection stops the city system from backing up into your home

What each item is made of decides that. Hard, non-porous items such as metal, glass, sealed tile and solid plastic can usually be cleaned and disinfected. Porous materials that contacted sewage, including carpet, padding, drywall, insulation, mattresses and upholstered furniture, have to be discarded. What a Professional Brings to Sewage Cleanup

This is one cleanup you should never take on yourself. The biological hazards in raw sewage require specialized training, proper personal protective equipment (Tyvek suits, respirators, protective gloves and boots) and EPA-registered disinfection products that are not available to consumers. Exposure to sewage pathogens can cause serious illness, including gastrointestinal disease, hepatitis, respiratory infections and skin infections.

Professional sewage cleanup also requires proper disposal of contaminated materials as biohazard waste. Putting carpet, insulation or drywall soaked with sewage out with household trash breaks the law and endangers sanitation workers and your community. A certified restoration company follows proper disposal protocols and works with licensed facilities that accept biohazard waste.

Signs You Need Sewage Cleanup in Woodbury

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Dark residue from sewage left on the basement floor or ringing a drain
  • More than one drain in the house overflowing or backing up together
  • People in the household getting stomach or intestinal symptoms with no clear cause
  • Tubs, toilets or floor drains pushing black water back into the room
  • Lower levels or drain openings giving off a heavy sewage odor, sometimes like rotten eggs
  • Toilets or drains that gurgle whenever you run water somewhere else
  • Wastewater seeping from the sewer cleanout or pooling in the yard above the line
