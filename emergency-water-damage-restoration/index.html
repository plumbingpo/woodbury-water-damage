Emergency Water Damage Restoration Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Shut off the water at the main whenever you leave home for more than a few days.
  • Save an emergency restoration company's number in your contacts ahead of time.
  • Know where your electrical panel is so power to wet rooms can be cut safely.
  • Sinks, dishwashers, washing machines and water heaters are all good spots for a leak sensor.
  • Swap worn hoses feeding the washer, sinks and toilets rather than waiting for a rupture.
  • Important paperwork and valuables belong above floor level, most of all on the lowest story.

When to Call for Emergency Water Damage Restoration in Woodbury

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • You see water escaping around a light fitting, from a wall outlet or through the ceiling.
  • You have no way to locate or get to the shutoff while water continues to pour.
  • Nobody was home when it started, so the length of the leak is unknown.
  • The tank on your water heater gave out and its contents are spreading through the room.
  • A ceiling is sagging or bulging with trapped water above it.
  • Standing water is spreading across floors toward other rooms or down the stairs.
  • Water has reached the electrical panel, appliances or power strips on the floor.

Emergency Water Damage Restoration: What Homeowners Ask

Treat it as an emergency if water is still flowing, is near wiring or outlets, involves sewage, is coming through a ceiling, or has soaked carpet, drywall or wood flooring. Minor spills on a tile floor that dry out fully can be handled without a rush call. Call whenever you have doubts. We would rather tell you it can wait than have you guess.
Start with a restoration crew whenever water has gotten into flooring, walls or a ceiling. On arrival our crew isolates or closes the supply, then starts extraction immediately. The lasting fix to the pipe belongs to a plumber, whose work can generally proceed during drying. Standing water does more damage each hour, so do not let it sit until the repair.
Price is set mostly by the square footage affected, what category the water is and the equipment needed. Some after-hours calls carry an added charge, and we will tell you about any before we send a crew. Shut off the water at the main valve if you can reach it safely. Turn off power to wet rooms at the panel, but never step in standing water to do it.
